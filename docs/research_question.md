RESEARCH QUESTION

How effectively can unsupervised machine-learning methods detect spatial-temporal anomalies in public transport GPS trajectories, and how can these anomalies support operational risk identification in urban transport systems?

Research aim

To develop and evaluate an automated anomaly-detection approach using unsupervised machine-learning methods on historical Klaipėda public transport GPS data, and to examine how the detected irregularities can support operational risk identification.

Research objectives
1. Analyse existing research on spatial-temporal anomaly detection, unsupervised machine learning and threat intelligence in urban transport.
2. Examine and preprocess historical Klaipėda public transport GPS data to reconstruct trajectories and extract relevant movement features.
3. Implement automated anomaly-detection methods, focusing on Isolation Forest and DBSCAN.
4. Evaluate and compare the methods in terms of anomaly-detection performance, computational efficiency and suitability for transport telemetry.
5. Analyse detected anomalies and assess their potential usefulness for operational risk identification.
